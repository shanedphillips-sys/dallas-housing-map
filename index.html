About & Sources

Interactive map of Dallas-area housing, zoning, land use, value, jobs, and demographics, for the Greater Dallas Planning Council.

Data sources

  • Parcels, zoning, land use, value, FAR, decade built — DCAD 2025 Certified + Collin & Denton CAD (DCAD)
  • Building permits (2000–2024) — City of Dallas
  • Demographics, rent & home-value change — U.S. Census ACS 5-year (+ Zillow ZHVI/ZORI)
  • Population / housing-unit change — Census Decennial (2010, 2020)
  • Job density — Census LODES (2022)
  • Expected adult earnings — Opportunity Insights
  • Building footprints (3D) — Microsoft ML footprints + OSM heights
  • Streets, alleys, parking, water — OpenStreetMap
  • Transit network — DART GTFS
  • Subsidized housing — TDHCA LIHTC inventory
  • Floodplain — FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer

Search © OpenStreetMap / Photon. Full per-layer methods are documented in DATA_SOURCES.md in the project repository.
